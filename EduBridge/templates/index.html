Core Innovation

The Barrier-to-Action Engine

Instead of giving you another boring list of deadlines, we diagnose why you haven't applied and create an actionable strategy to overcome that exact obstacle.

⚖️

Eligibility is confusing

Breaks dense legalese into an interactive 3-way checklist: [Met], [Not Met], or [Unsure], with clear next steps for every unsure item.

→ Yes/No Verification Grid
📂

I don't know which documents are needed

Shows exact certificates, issuing authorities (Tahsildar, Principal, DigiLocker), size constraints, and DigiLocker shortcuts.

→ Paperwork Pipeline
⏰

I discovered it late

Emergency triage sprint prioritizing minimum viable submission tasks, provisional marksheets, and next-cycle reminders.

→ Fast-Track Triage Plan
📶

I have limited internet or device access

Downloadable 100% offline text checklist (.txt) and a 15-minute Cyber-Café/College Lab upload battle plan.

→ Offline Text Pack
🧭

I don't know how to start

Gentle 3-phase roadmap: 10-minute zero-pressure warmup, fill-in-the-blank SOP template, and launch review.

→ 3-Phase Starter Roadmap
⏱️

I have limited time

Time-boxes the application into four 10-15 minute micro-sprints with an interactive focus timer on the page.

→ 15-Min Micro-Sprints
